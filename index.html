QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

Do I need any insurance background?

No. Some of you are freight forwarders, some work in export or import, some in insurance or broking. You will meet the same problem from different sides, and that is the point.

What exactly will my team be doing?

You are an insurance company. Your client is shipping a reefer of fresh cherries from Lleida to Dubai and nobody has insured it yet. Over four afternoons you read the risk, choose the cover, price it, issue the policy, keep the cargo file — and on Thursday something goes wrong and you settle the claim on the terms you chose on Tuesday.

What will I have to produce?

Seven numbered pieces of evidence, one per working block: the risk assessment and matrix, the cover decision and quotation, the payment confirmation, the certificate of insurance, the cargo report with the milestones and route note, the claim and its assessment, and the indemnity calculation with a memo to the client. Together they become the appendix of your team dossier — which is why you never write the dossier from scratch.

Why does it matter which browser I use?

TT Circle saves your case file in the browser, not on a server. If you switch machine or clear your browsing data, your policy number stops appearing automatically and you have to type it in by hand. We keep a copy, but you lose ten minutes you will not have.

What is MERCATOR?

The in-game currency. Each team starts with 60. Hints cost 5 or 10, depending on how much of the thinking they do for you. Whatever you have left breaks a tie on the leaderboard — so a hint should feel like a decision, not a button.

How am I assessed?

Team dossier 50%, individual exam 40%, participation 10%. The dossier’s conclusions alone carry 40% of the dossier mark. Your points do not depend on whether your claim pays — they depend on the quality of your decisions and on doing the work.

What do I need to receive the certificate?

Three things. Attendance: at least three of the four afternoons, which is 75% of the course. Assessment: an overall mark of 60% or above across the dossier, the exam and participation. Delivery: your team’s dossier submitted with its seven pieces of evidence.

Miss more than one afternoon and we cannot certify the hours, whatever your marks look like. If you know in advance that you will miss a session, tell us before it happens rather than after.

What if my claim does not pay anything on Thursday?

That is a result, not a failure, and it costs you no points. Write the zero, name the clause that causes it, and say what you would have bought on Tuesday and what it would have cost. That paragraph scores higher than a settlement nobody explained.

Can I catch up if I miss an afternoon?

Partly. Each block produces one piece of evidence that the next one needs, so a gap is felt. Tell us as early as you can and we will get your team’s file back on track before the next session.

Something is not working. What do I do?

Write to us before the session rather than during it. A platform problem at 17:00 on a Tuesday costs your team a whole block. The same problem on Sunday costs nothing.
