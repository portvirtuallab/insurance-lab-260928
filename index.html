THE FILE ON YOUR DESK

One shipment, four afternoons

Fruits del Segrià SL in Lleida is shipping fresh cherries to Gulf Fresh Trading FZE in Dubai. Nobody has insured it yet. That is why they came to you.

CargoFresh cherries · 1 × 40' reefer · 20 pallets · 21,600 kg gross
TemperatureSet point +2 °C · alarms at +1 °C and +4 °C
RouteBarcelona → transhipment at Salalah → Jebel Ali
Transit25 days door to door
Invoice value / freight72,000 EUR / 3,800 EUR

Over the four afternoons you will assess the risk, choose the cover, calculate the premium, activate the policy, keep the cargo file up to date — and on Thursday something will go wrong, and you will settle the claim.

Whatever you decide on Tuesday, you live with on Thursday. The claim is settled under the cover you chose two days earlier. Nobody can hand you the right answer halfway through, because the right answer depends on what you bought.

HOW YOU ARE ASSESSED

Dossier 50% · exam 40% · participation 10%

Every working block ends with one numbered piece of evidence. Seven in total, from the risk matrix to the settled claim, and they become the appendix of your team dossier. You do not write the dossier on Thursday evening — you assemble it, from work you have already done.

The PVL GAME leaderboard is a separate thing: 1,000 points across nineteen challenges. It rewards pace and completeness, and it does not decide your grade.
